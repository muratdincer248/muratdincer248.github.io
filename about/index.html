CV

Twelve years, three chapters. Advertising taught me to tell a story in a single frame. Consulting at Userspots and IBM iX taught me to design for millions. Delivery Hero taught me to build the foundations other teams design on. Today I bring all three into how I work, and lately into code as well.

Always exploring, mostly in Figma and increasingly in code.

Experience

  1. Delivery Hero

    Senior UI Designer → Lead Interaction Designer | May 2021 – Present

    Delivery Hero marked the next chapter in my journey, where I began applying everything I’d learned about product design, systems thinking, and leadership at a global scale. I started by leading the visual design of Armor, a B2B design system supporting 50+ products, before helping bring multiple design systems together into Cape, a shared foundation now powering 85+ platforms across global brands.

    Beyond building scalable systems, my focus expanded to creating alignment between people, products, and teams. From leading a team of five designers and shaping multi-brand Help Center experiences to driving design system adoption, community initiatives, and AI-assisted workflows, this chapter has strengthened my belief that great design is as much about enabling others as it is about creating great experiences.

  2. IBM iX

    Senior UI Designer → Visual Design Lead | Jan 2018 – Apr 2021

    IBM iX was where I moved from designing individual products to thinking about entire digital ecosystems. Working primarily with Turkcell, I contributed to over 30 digital products across B2C and B2B platforms, gaining a deeper understanding of how design consistency, scalability, and business needs intersect.

    My role evolved into design leadership, leading a team of 10 UI designers across multiple squads and driving design system initiatives for Turkcell, Halkbank, and the Abu Dhabi government. Along the way, I mentored designers, delivered internal training to IBM design teams both locally and globally, and learned how to build scalable design foundations while bringing people and teams together around a shared vision.

  3. Userspots

    UI/UX Designer | Sep 2015 – Jan 2018

    Userspots shaped my foundation as a UX/UI designer. Working on end-to-end design projects across banking, e-commerce, retail, telecommunications, travel, and consumer technology, I gained experience designing digital products for brands such as Hepsiburada, Beymen, Migros, QNB Finansbank, Pegasus Airlines, and Vestel. This diversity helped me develop a versatile, user-centered approach to design.

    I also worked as a lecturer at Userspots Academy, leading UX/UI workshops and training sessions for corporate teams and individual learners. This experience strengthened my communication, mentorship, and knowledge-sharing skills, laying the groundwork for my later leadership roles.

How I work

Key principles

  1. 01

    Start with people. Set the direction.

    I connect user needs with business goals to focus teams on the right problems. The best experiences make even complex challenges feel simple.

  2. 02

    Connect people. Create alignment.

    I bring design, engineering, and business together around a shared vision, turning different perspectives into better decisions and collective ownership.

  3. 03

    Experiment early. Move with purpose.

    I use prototypes, code, and AI to challenge assumptions, reduce uncertainty, and help teams make confident decisions before investing in solutions.

  4. 04

    Build foundations. Multiply impact.

    I create scalable systems, shared practices, and tools that empower teams to move faster, maintain quality, and build on each other’s work.
