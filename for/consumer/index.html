What I do

I design products used by millions every day. At Delivery Hero I lead interaction design for Cape, the central design system behind our brands across the globe. Before that I shaped Turkcell’s digital experience at IBM iX, and through my independent practice I built gamified campaigns for Samsung, Coca-Cola, Unilever and Anadolu Efes. Lately I spend as much time in Cursor and Claude Code as in Figma, building the AI tools my team uses every day. And my favorite side quest: Tipza.
