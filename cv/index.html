Murat Dinçer.

Product designer · Design systems · AI tools for teams
Berlin, Germany · dincer.design
murat@dincer.design+49 152 24914879de.linkedin.com/in/mrtdincer

Product designer with 12+ years shaping consumer apps used by millions. At Delivery Hero I lead interaction design for Cape, the multi-brand design system behind our consumer and back-office products across 10 brands and 85+ platforms, and I work hand in hand with product designers on end-to-end experiences, not only components and tokens. With the Help Center team I shaped the multi-brand Help Center, a modular product that runs natively inside every host app. Before that I led visual design across Turkcell’s consumer apps at IBM iX and designed Fiat’s connected-car app. I bring cross-functional leadership, systems thinking and AI-native workflows to consumer products at scale.

  • Design systems, multi-brand theming, motion tokens
  • User research, prototyping and user testing
  • Workshops, stakeholder alignment, mentoring
  • Visual, interaction and motion design; accessibility
  • AI workflows: Figma MCP, Claude Code, Cursor
